# todo/: what makes Crucible hard to copy

Working notes on how Crucible stands apart from the AI-board products that already exist, and what would make it hard to copy. Tasks for the Sun Nov 8 deadline stay in [`../TODO.md`](../TODO.md). This folder is the longer game, and it doubles as material for the video's 16-week plan.

## The honest starting point
- **The idea is already taken.** [Ask the AI Council](https://asktheaicouncil.com/) sells a Chairman plus four AI advisors to small businesses ($29.99/month, iPhone app, spoken voices). At least four other AI products are called "Crucible", and several multi-AI debate products are called "Quorum". See [`01-competitors.md`](01-competitors.md).
- **Prompts, UI and the five-seat format can be copied in days.** Anyone can call the same Claude models through OpenRouter.
- **What a copier can't get quickly:** proof that the board catches real risks, a reviewed rule base, outcome data from real decisions, a verified AI-tool catalog, and trusted referral partners. Those are the barriers, in [`03-barriers.md`](03-barriers.md).
