Four seats at the table

Each advisor has one job, so every side of your decision is argued by someone committed to it.

Devil’s Advocate

Argues against whatever you’re leaning toward, so you hear the strongest case for the other side.

Strategist

Looks past this month and asks what the choice sets up for next year.

Realist

Checks the plan against the time, money and people you actually have.

Chairman

Hears all three, names where they agree, and writes the decision.

How a session runs

You bring the question. The board works through it in rounds, and every round stays visible, so you can see how the decision was made.

  1. 1

    Ask

    Describe the decision in your own words. Add what’s at stake and any numbers you have.

  2. 2

    Opening arguments

    Each advisor takes a position and makes its case.

  3. 3

    Cross-examination

    Advisors answer each other directly. Weak arguments get dropped.

  4. 4

    Verdict

    The Chairman writes one decision, plus the conditions that would change it.
