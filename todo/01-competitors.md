# Competitors

Checked Nov 7, 2026. Check again before the Nov 17 finals; these products change monthly.

## Direct competitor: Ask the AI Council
[asktheaicouncil.com](https://asktheaicouncil.com/) is the closest match: "a Chairman and four specialist AI advisors" for small businesses, "on iPhone and web."

| | Ask the AI Council | Crucible |
|---|---|---|
| Board | Chairman + 4 advisors split by department: operations, growth, innovation, finance | Chairman + 5 seats split by job: Industry Expert, AI-Tools Expert, Realist, Strategist, Devil's Advocate. A Researcher gathers facts first. |
| Built-in opposition | Each advisor argues from its own department | The Devil's Advocate must argue against the owner's leaning in Round 1 and against the board's consensus in Round 2 |
| Evidence | "Agent research runs" on paid plans (2 a month on Pro). The home page doesn't say whether claims are sourced. | Every run starts with web research. Each fact is marked verified (with a source link) or unverified, and seats cite facts by ID. |
| Legal and compliance | No legal advisor among the four | The Realist runs pass / flag / block checks on legal, compliance, money, operations and goals |
| Verdict | The Chairman decides, with reasons and next actions | The Chairman *recommends* proceed / pilot / defer / reject, with a confidence %, the advisors who disagreed, conditions, dated next steps, and who to escalate to (e.g., an attorney). The owner accepts or overrides, and an override needs a note. |
| AI tools | AI and tool decisions are led by the innovation advisor | A dedicated AI-Tools Expert names real products with use, cost, integrations and caveats |
| Proof of accuracy | None on the home page | A 10-scenario trap test, board vs. a single prompt (full results pending) |
| **What they have that we don't** | Spoken voices, iPhone app, memory across sessions, follow-ups on past decisions, Spanish, accounts and billing (Pro $29.99/mo, Pro Business $69.99/mo), free for students with a .edu email | |

Don't race them on features. Win on proof and trust; see [`03-barriers.md`](03-barriers.md).

## Same idea, general audience
These multi-AI debate products aren't built for small businesses and have no compliance checks:
- **Products named Quorum:** [askquorum.ai](https://www.askquorum.ai/) (a structured 7-phase process: gather, debate, vote…), [aiquorum.ai](https://aiquorum.ai/) (a panel of models from OpenAI, Anthropic and Google), [elftech.ai/quorum](https://www.elftech.ai/quorum), and an open-source framework, [Solvely-Colin/Quorum](https://github.com/Solvely-Colin/Quorum).
- **Products named Crucible in the same space:**
  - [crucible.business](https://crucible.business/) ("One Founder. Six AI Partners.")
  - [crucible.soupup.ai](https://crucible.soupup.ai/about) (startup-idea assessment, "Test the idea before you build it")
  - [ai-crucible.com](https://ai-crucible.com/articles/ai-crucible-official-launch/) (a multi-model platform)
  - Crucible by [Roundtable Labs](https://crucible.roundtablelabs.ai/integrations) (closing Aug 31, 2026)

  A name this crowded isn't a brand barrier.

## Platforms that could absorb the feature
- **Claude for Small Business (Anthropic)** works inside QuickBooks, PayPal, HubSpot and other tools: 43 workflows and 900,000+ installs as of [Sept 15, 2026](https://claude.com/resources/articles/claude-for-small-business-launches-new-workflows-integrations-and-training-programs). Its rule is "you approve before anything sends, posts, or pays" ([launch post](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-for-small-business)). It *does* the work; it doesn't convene a board on *whether* to do it.
  - **Our positioning:** Crucible is the check before an owner lets an AI agent act.
  - **Risk:** a platform like this adds a "second opinion" mode.
- **General chatbots** (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) give one answer, free, that tends to agree with how the question was framed.
- **AI-tool directories and review sites** rate a tool in general, not whether it fits one particular business.
